Colour converter (HEX, RGB, HSL)

Paste a CSS colour in any common format and get the others, plus its contrast with white and black.

Runs locally: your input never leaves this page.

~/bitbench/color-converter local

Accepted formats

  • HEX: #rgb, #rgba, #rrggbb, #rrggbbaa (the # is optional).
  • RGB: rgb(59, 76, 202), rgb(59 76 202 / 0.5), percentages allowed.
  • HSL: hsl(233 57% 51%), hsla(233, 57%, 51%, 0.5).

Named colours (blue, rebeccapurple…) and newer spaces (oklch, lab, color()) are not converted.

Contrast

The contrast ratio follows WCAG 2: from 1:1 (no contrast) to 21:1 (black on white). Normal text needs at least 4.5:1 (level AA) or 7:1 (AAA); large text needs 3:1. Transparency is ignored for the contrast calculation.

Rounding

HSL values are rounded to one decimal, so converting HSL back to HEX can differ by one unit on a channel.
