Base64 encode and decode

Convert text to Base64 and back, with accents and emoji handled correctly.

Runs locally: your input never leaves this page.

Why UTF-8 matters

Base64 encodes bytes, not characters. This tool first converts text to UTF-8 bytes, so “é” becomes w6k= and “👋” 8J+Riw==. The browser’s built-in btoa() alone fails on such characters or produces Latin-1 bytes that other systems decode wrongly.

Base64URL

URL-safe Base64 replaces + with - and / with _, and usually drops the = padding. It is used in JWTs and URLs. When decoding, both variants are accepted, and line breaks or spaces are ignored.

Errors are reported, not guessed

  • Invalid characters are named.
  • Impossible lengths (4n+1 characters) are rejected.
  • If the decoded bytes are not valid UTF-8 text (an image, a zip file…), the tool says so instead of showing garbled text.

Base64 is not encryption

Anyone can decode Base64. Never use it to hide passwords or secrets.
