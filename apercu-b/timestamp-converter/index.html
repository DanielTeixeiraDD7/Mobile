Unix timestamp converter

Convert between Unix timestamps and human-readable dates, in UTC and in your own time zone.

End with Z for UTC or an offset like +02:00. Without one, the date is read in your time zone (UTC).

Seconds or milliseconds?

Unix timestamps count from 1 January 1970 at 00:00:00 UTC. Most systems use seconds (1700000000), JavaScript and many APIs use milliseconds (1700000000000). In “Detect” mode, values of 1011 or more are read as milliseconds (1011 seconds would be in the year 5138). Force the unit if you need to.

Time zones

A timestamp has no time zone: it is the same instant everywhere. Only its display changes. 1700000000 is 22:13:20 on 14 November 2023 in UTC, 23:13:20 in Paris (UTC+1 in winter) and 22:13:20 in Lisbon. The local time shown here uses your browser’s time zone.

Date to timestamp

Write the date in ISO 8601. End it with Z (UTC) or an offset like +02:00 to be unambiguous; without one, it is read in your local time zone, which may differ from the server that will use the timestamp. Impossible dates such as 30 February are rejected.

Limits

Dates must be within ±100 million days of 1970 (the JavaScript Date range). Leap seconds are not represented in Unix time.
